Is it safe? Could I lose a photo I wanted to keep?
Frankie never deletes anything on its own. You decide what goes. When you do delete photos, they're moved to your Photos app's Recently Deleted album, where they stay recoverable for 30 days — nothing is gone forever until then. Frankie also never lets you delete the last remaining copy of a photo.
How do I recover a photo I deleted?
Open the Photos app, go to Albums, scroll down to Recently Deleted, select the photos, and tap Recover. Photos remain there for 30 days after deletion.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. All of Frankie's analysis happens on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or anyone else, there's no account to create, and there's no tracking. Your library stays private to you.
How does the free trial and pricing work?
Frankie is free to download and includes a 7-day free trial with full access. After the trial you can unlock everything with a one-time purchase or an annual subscription, or continue using a limited free version. All purchases are handled securely by Apple through the App Store.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account, not inside Frankie. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, and you can view, change, or cancel from there.
How do I restore a purchase on a new device?
Open Frankie, go to the paywall or Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with, your access will be restored.
Frankie grouped some photos that don't look like duplicates. Why?
Frankie groups photos that are visually similar or taken as part of the same moment. Occasionally it may group photos more loosely than you'd expect — you're always in full control, so simply keep whatever you want and Frankie will respect your choice. If you're seeing consistently odd groupings, please email us with an example so we can improve it.
Frankie needs photo access — why, and how do I change it?
Frankie needs access to your photo library so it can find duplicates and help you tidy up. You can review or change this any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Photos.
