Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026
The short version: Frankie does its work entirely on your iPhone. Your photos, videos, and the analysis Frankie performs never leave your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. We do not have servers that receive your data, and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Frankie ("Frankie", "we", "us", "the app") handles your information. We've written it in plain language because we think you should be able to understand exactly what happens to your data. If anything here is unclear, please contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Your photos stay on your device

Frankie helps you find duplicate and similar photos, organise screenshots, and clean up your photo library. To do this, Frankie needs permission to access your photo library. When you grant that permission:

  • All analysis of your photos — including detecting duplicates, identifying similar images, grouping photos into moments, reading photo details such as sharpness, and recognising screenshots — happens on your device, using Apple's on-device technologies.
  • Your photos and videos are never uploaded to us, to any cloud service operated by us, or to any third party.
  • The results Frankie produces (such as which photos are duplicates, or your Library Score) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Frankie only ever deletes a photo when you explicitly choose to. Photos you delete are moved to your Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album, where they remain recoverable for 30 days, in line with how Apple's Photos app works.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Frankie has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics that identify you. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not build a profile of you.

Because Frankie runs on your device and does not send your data to us, we simply do not receive it.

Purchases

Frankie offers a free trial and paid options (a one-time purchase and an annual subscription). All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using Apple's in-app purchase system. Frankie does not see or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials — these are handled entirely by Apple.

Apple may share limited, non-identifying transaction information with us (such as confirmation that a purchase or subscription is valid) so the app can unlock the features you've paid for. This information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. To understand how Apple handles your purchase and account data, please review Apple's policy.

Photo library permission

Frankie requests access to your photo library solely to provide its features — finding duplicates, organising screenshots, and helping you tidy your collection. You can grant or revoke this permission at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. If you deny access, Frankie will not be able to analyse your library, but the app will explain this and offer to open Settings so you can change your choice.

Notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, Frankie may send you occasional reminders on your device (for example, about continuing a cleanup session). These are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Frankie does not use a remote notification server.

Data storage and security

All data Frankie creates — analysis results, your Library Score, achievement progress, and a small record of your deletion history — is stored locally on your device. Because this data does not leave your iPhone, it is protected by your device's own security, including your passcode and encryption. If you delete Frankie, this locally stored data is removed with it.

Children's privacy

Frankie is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because Frankie does not collect personal information from anyone, this is naturally the case for users of all ages.

Third parties

Frankie does not include third-party advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party tracking software. The only third party involved in your use of Frankie is Apple, in its role of operating the App Store and processing purchases, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, note the change within the app. Because Frankie's core commitment — that your photos stay on your device — is fundamental to how the app is built, we do not anticipate changes that would weaken it.
