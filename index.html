Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026
The short version: Frankie does its work entirely on your phone. Your photos and the analysis Frankie performs never leave your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. We do not have servers that receive your data, and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Frankie ("Frankie", "we", "us", "the app") handles your information. It covers Frankie for iPhone (available on the App Store) and Frankie for Android (available on Google Play). We've written it in plain language because we think you should be able to understand exactly what happens to your data. If anything here is unclear, please contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Your photos stay on your device

Frankie helps you find duplicate and similar photos, organise screenshots, and clean up your photo library. To do this, Frankie needs permission to access your photo library. When you grant that permission:

  • All analysis of your photos — including detecting duplicates, identifying similar images, grouping photos into moments, reading photo details such as sharpness, and recognising screenshots — happens on your device. On iPhone, Frankie uses Apple's on-device technologies. On Android, Frankie uses on-device image-analysis software from Google (ML Kit and MediaPipe) that runs inside the app on your phone.
  • Your photos are never uploaded to us, to any cloud service operated by us, or to any third party.
  • The results Frankie produces (such as which photos are duplicates, or your Library Score) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Frankie only ever deletes a photo when you explicitly choose to, and you confirm every deletion. On iPhone, photos you delete are moved to the Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album. On Android, they are moved to your phone's trash, and you can restore them from Recent Activity in Frankie. Either way, they remain recoverable for 30 days.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Frankie has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics that identify you. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not build a profile of you.

Because Frankie runs on your device and does not send your data to us, we simply do not receive it. On Android, the usage-statistics reporting that Google's ML Kit software includes by default has been switched off in Frankie.

Purchases

Frankie offers a 7-day free trial, a limited free version, and a single one-time purchase (Frankie Forever) that unlocks everything. There is no subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google through Google Play on Android. Frankie does not see or store your payment card details, billing address, or account credentials — these are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Apple or Google may share limited, non-identifying transaction information with the app (such as confirmation that a purchase is valid) so it can unlock the features you've paid for. This information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Photo library permission

Frankie requests access to your photo library solely to provide its features — finding duplicates, organising screenshots, and helping you tidy your collection. You can grant or revoke this permission at any time: on iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos, and on Android in your phone's Settings under Apps → Frankie → Permissions. If you deny access, Frankie will not be able to analyse your library, but the app will explain this and offer to open Settings so you can change your choice.

Data storage and security

All data Frankie creates — analysis results, your Library Score, achievement progress, and a small record of your deletion history (including small thumbnails of deleted photos) — is stored locally on your device. Because this data does not leave your phone, it is protected by your device's own security, including your passcode and encryption. If you delete Frankie, this locally stored data is removed with it.

On Android, only two small pieces of this data may be included in your phone's own backup to your Google account, if you have backup turned on: the start date of your free trial and your Library Score progress. No photos, analysis results, or deletion history are backed up.

Children's privacy

Frankie is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because Frankie does not collect personal information from anyone, this is naturally the case for users of all ages.

Third parties

Frankie does not include third-party advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party tracking software. The only third parties involved in your use of Frankie are Apple and Google, in their roles of operating the App Store and Google Play and processing purchases, and Google's on-device image-analysis software on Android, which runs on your phone and does not send your photos anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, note the change within the app. Because Frankie's core commitment — that your photos stay on your device — is fundamental to how the app is built, we do not anticipate changes that would weaken it.
